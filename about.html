About me

Hey there and welcome to my art portfolio website! I'm W_AsteOfSpace or W_Aste.

My art consists of primarily digital art with some traditional art mixed in. When I do traditional sketches I tend to do them in black pen.

You can usually define my artstyle by it's simplistic lineart and color style. In a lot of my digital sketches you can also usually find simple dotted eyes and pencil-like brush.

You can also commission me to create artworks for you through this website!
